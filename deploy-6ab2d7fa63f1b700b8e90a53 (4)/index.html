WHO WE ARE

About Children's Hope Junior School

In God We Trust

Children's Hope Junior School offers quality education at a lower, affordable tuition rate so that orphans and vulnerable children in Namukooge and surrounding communities can still sit in a classroom, not in a sugarcane plantation, not beside a charcoal kiln, and not on the street.

After COVID-19 disrupted livelihoods, and after a long dry season that dried gardens and left families without food, many children had nowhere to turn. Some went to cut sugarcane. Others burned charcoal. Some begged on the streets just to get one or two meals a day. The HIV/AIDS crisis has also left many children without parents. About 95% of our pupils are total orphans. Without support, education would have remained a dream.

How the School Began: A Story for Our Partners

One afternoon, two men, Mr. Lazarus Semujju and Mr. Moses Kusemererwa, sat together and looked at the children around them. They saw empty schoolbags, tired faces, and young hands that should have held pencils but were instead holding machetes and charcoal bags.

They asked themselves a simple question: “If we do not open a door for these children, who will?”

That conversation became a decision. They pooled what little they had, found a wooden structure in Namukooge, and opened Children's Hope Junior School: a place where every child, especially the orphaned and the poorest, could receive quality teaching, dignity, and hope at a tuition rate families can still afford.

Today the school continues that mission. We keep fees as low as possible. We welcome day and boarding pupils (boarding from Primary level). Our classrooms are still simple wooden structures. We need better buildings, desks, textbooks, water, games equipment, and other basic school facilities. We invite friends of the school (individuals, churches, and organisations) to walk with us.

How you can help a child stay in school

  • Pay tuition for an orphan for one term or one year
  • Provide school requirements: books, pens, mathematical sets, bags
  • Sponsor a uniform so a child can attend with dignity
  • Support meals or boarding for children who have no home to return to
  • Help with infrastructure: classroom construction, desks, textbooks, safe water, games and sports equipment, toilets, and other facilities every proper school needs

Every shilling goes directly to keeping a pupil in class and improving the learning environment. Your gift is not charity alone: it is an investment in a generation that COVID-19, HIV/AIDS, and drought nearly erased from the classroom.

School Requirements

Items marked for boarders are in addition to the day-pupil list.

Nursery (Baby to Top) : Day

  • School uniform (as guided by the school)
  • 1 dozen exercise books (48 pages)
  • Pencils, rubber, sharpener
  • Crayons / coloured pencils
  • School bag
  • Handkerchief / tissue
  • Toilet paper (5 rolls)
  • Ream of paper (where required)

Primary 1 to 4 : Day

  • School uniform and sports wear
  • Exercise books (as per subject list)
  • Pens, pencils, rubber, ruler
  • Mathematical set (from P.3)
  • Graph book (from Primary 4)
  • School bag
  • English dictionary (simple)
  • Toilet paper (5 rolls)

Primary 5 to 7 : Day

  • Full school uniform and sports wear
  • Enough exercise books per subject
  • Mathematical set
  • Graph books and counter books
  • English dictionary and atlas (P.6 to P.7)
  • School bag
  • Ream of paper / manila (as required)
  • Toilet paper (5 rolls)

Primary Boarders (P.1 to P.7) : Extra

  • Mattress, 2 pairs of bed sheets, blanket
  • Mosquito net
  • Towel, soap, toothpaste, toothbrush, jelly
  • Basin / bucket, cup, plate, spoon
  • 2 pairs of shoes and slippers
  • Night wear / home clothes (modest)
  • Trunk or suitcase for belongings
  • Toilet paper (10 rolls)

Requirements may be adjusted slightly by class teachers at the start of each term. Families who cannot afford full requirements are encouraged to speak to the directors : no child should stay home because of missing items. Sponsors are welcome to cover tuition, uniform, or requirements for any pupil.
